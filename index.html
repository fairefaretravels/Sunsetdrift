Sunset Drift

Bomb the hill on a longboard. Dodge traffic, grab bits, and drift for boosts.

Steer: arrows or A/D, or drag on screen.
Drift: hold Space or Shift. Jump: W or Up.
2 Player: P1 A/D, W, S · P2 arrows (↓ drifts)

Ride: arrows or A/D, drift with Space/Shift. Explore: use A/D or arrows to walk, E or Interact near a location.
