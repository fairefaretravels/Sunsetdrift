Sunset Slide

Bomb the hill on a longboard. Dodge traffic, grab bits, and drift for boosts.

Steer: arrows or A/D, or drag on screen.
Drift: hold Space or Shift. Jump: W or Up.

2 players (keyboard): P1 arrows, Up jumps, Down drifts. P2 A/D, W jumps, S drifts.

Release a long drift to fire a boost. Three crashes and the ride ends.
